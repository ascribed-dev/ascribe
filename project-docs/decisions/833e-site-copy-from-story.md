section: The website
---

**The site's copy comes from [the brand story](../brand/story.md).** Its headline, the sentence that says what Ascribe is, the pillars, the voice, and its words. The site names no other tool, and doesn't say "help authoring tool", "platform", or a superlative. A page that needs a sentence the story doesn't support changes the story first. _Why:_ one story keeps every page saying the same thing, and the story is where the reasons for its words are kept. _From:_ the maintainer, 21 October 2026; [the website plan](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/blob/58afc492/project-docs/website/README.md#decisions), decision 4.
