section: The look
---

**The pages' colors are Deep River and Shallows, and their headings are the system's serif.** Every shared `color.*` token in `design/tokens.toml` takes a step of the identity's hues, so the element library, review, the HTML report, the archive, the Astro toolbar, the docs site, and the VS Code preview's fallbacks share them. Deep is the neutrals: Shallows' `#f2f7f9` ground and white surface in light, Deep River's `#0b1824` ground and `#112233` surface in dark. River is the accent: links and primary buttons, review's among them, a note, steps, and review's changed. Moss is a tip, availability's GA, and review's added. Amber is focus rings and outlines only, and the flash's tint. A review thread and a tab under the pointer take `color.panel`, a step of deep that shows on a white page as on the ground. The other hues are derived and checked in `design/candidates/pairs.toml`: magenta for an important note and review's moved, a crimson red for a caution and review's removed, and a yellow apart from amber for a warning. Headings on the site and in the report take `font.heading`, `ui-serif, Charter, "Bitstream Charter", Cambria, Georgia, serif`, a system font, so decision 48 holds. _Why:_ the mark's colors were the brand's and the pages' were still indigo; one palette makes every surface Ascribe draws read as one product, and serif headings are the identity's reading face without shipping a font. Moss leans yellow-green, so red and violet were moved until each pair a reader sees side by side stays about as far apart for red-green color blindness as the indigo palette's did. _From:_ the maintainer, choosing board 11 on 21 October 2026 ([the identity](../brand/identity.md)), and approving the derived values on 22 October 2026; [the website plan](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/blob/58afc492/project-docs/website/README.md#decisions), decisions 1, 5, 6, and 11, carried out in #816.
