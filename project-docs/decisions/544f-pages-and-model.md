section: The binary
---

**Pages and Content model act on their entries from menus and title-bar filters; Insert at the Cursor is for a phrase or a widget only, and nothing is dragged from Content model into a page.** In Pages, each page and fragment carries its file, so VS Code colors it by its problems as the Explorer does, and a page the build you're looking at leaves out is dimmed, from the builds `ascribe/inventory` names for each page. A page's menu has Open Preview to the Side, also its button, Copy Link, Rename or Move…, Find All References, and Reveal in Explorer, and a section's has New Page Here; **Orphans Only** filters the view, in place of a group of orphans. In Content model, page types come first, each with how many pages have it, each dimension lists its values, and an entry's count is what Find All References lists for it. Its menu has Find All References, Rename… on a phrase or a dimension's value, through the server's rename and the refactor preview, and **Insert at the Cursor** on a phrase or a widget, which runs Insert a phrase or Insert a widget with it chosen, in the project's last Markdown editor, which the extension tracks; where that cursor can't take it, it gives the action's hint and changes nothing. **Unused Only** filters the view. Dragging pages within Pages to reorder them is the navigation plan's. _Why:_ phrases and widgets are the two kinds inserted by name, and a drag would do what the menu does, for more work; VS Code's active editor is none while the preview or another webview has focus, so the editor to write into has to be remembered. _From:_ the maintainer, on the editor's views plan ([README](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/blob/395fe80d/project-docs/editor-ui/README.md), decisions 5 and 17) and its [proposal](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/blob/395fe80d/project-docs/proposals/editor-ui.md#settled), settled 16 October 2026; shipped in [#587](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/pull/587), 17 October 2026; recorded when the plan closed, with [#544](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/544).
