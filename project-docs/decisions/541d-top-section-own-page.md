section: The language
---

**A page beside a top folder is read with that folder's section, as its own page, and not among the content root's pages.** For `guides.md` beside `guides/`, the content root's group in a sidebar, its reading order, and `llms.txt`'s first section have the home page and the root's pages that aren't beside a folder; `guides.md` is first in the Guides group, and is read, in previous and next, an archive, `llms.txt`, and the agents' index, before the rest of that section (SPEC §7.8, "Reading order"; the navigation contract, §3). `nav.json` and `ascribe model` still have the page in the root's `pages` and as the section's `page`, as a page beside any nested folder is (decision [#537b](537b-navigation-output.md)), and a reader groups it so. Below the root nothing changes: a page beside its folder is among its parent's pages, with the nested section right after it. This says which group the page is read with, not where its section comes among the top sections, which [#610](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/610) settles. _Why:_ a page beside a top folder is that section's own page, so a reader expects it at the head of its group, and read in a line, the sidebar's groups and previous and next have to agree. _From:_ the maintainer, on the navigation plan ([README](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/blob/395fe80d/project-docs/navigation/README.md), decisions 4, 7, and 24), merging [#584](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/pull/584), 17 October 2026, which changed SPEC §7.8 and amended decision [#539d](539d-neighbors.md) to say so; recorded when the plan closed, with [#541](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/541).
