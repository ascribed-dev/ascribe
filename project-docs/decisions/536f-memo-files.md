---
section: The language
---

**A memo file is a Markdown file in `memos/`, beside `ascribe.toml`, with two frontmatter keys, `about` and `quote`, about one thing, and its place is worked out each time it's read: placed, outdated, or detached.** `about`, required, names what the memo is about as `ascribe refs` names a target: a page or fragment by its content path, a heading as `page.md#id`, named in the fragment when it's in one, or `phrase:`, `feature:`, `term:`, `dimension:`, `note:`, or `widget:`; a memo about `term:<id>` is about the term, whatever carries it, which since [decision #545a](545a-glossary-pages.md) is its heading on a glossary page. `quote`, optional, narrows the memo to the first block, in source order, of the page or the heading's section whose source as written holds it, whitespace runs read as one space and case kept; a quote across two blocks isn't found, and there's no similarity score. A memo is placed on what `about` names, or on the block its quote is in; outdated when no block holds its quote, and then it stays on the page or section; and detached when `about` names nothing. Nothing is written back to the file. The body is CommonMark, shown as written, with nothing in it resolved, and a memo has no author, no date, no build, and no line. Every `.md` file in `memos/` and its subfolders is a memo, a name that starts with `.` skipped; a file's name and its folder mean nothing, and the subfolders are the team's to arrange. `memos/` can't be inside the content root. The folder is fixed, as `.ascribe/memos/` is ([decision #536j](536j-personal-memos.md)); a key with those defaults would be additive if a project ever needs to move them. Memo files are read through `ascribe_resolve::FileSystem`. Renaming a page, moving it, or changing a heading's id, with `ascribe rename` or in the editor (`F2`, or moving a file in the Explorer), rewrites the `about` of each memo that names it, in both folders, keeping its `#id` when the page moves, and leaves its `quote` and text alone. SPEC §7.7 states these as the language's rules, for the reason [decision #533](533-memo-files-in-spec.md) gives. _Why:_ a reason longer than a line or two, or about a whole page or a content model entry, has no place to stand in the page; working its place out each time, with no stored position to go stale, means the checks say when the words it quotes changed or what it names went, and a rename keeps it where it was. A memo about several things would need each state for each of them, and a quote that could match in several places. _From:_ the maintainer, on the memos plan ([README](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/blob/395fe80d/project-docs/memos/README.md), decisions 11 to 14, 24, and 26, the last two from [phase 1](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/blob/395fe80d/project-docs/memos/phase-1-decisions.md), and [phase 6](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/blob/395fe80d/project-docs/memos/phase-6-editor.md)'s rename), 14 and 15 October 2026, as [#557](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/pull/557) and [#561](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/pull/561) shipped them on 17 October 2026; recorded when the plan closed, with [#536](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/536).
