section: The binary
---

**The Project view's sections are Actions, then Health, then About, and Health's counts are in groups by when each is from: Every build, As you type, and At the last save.** This replaces two choices of [the Project view proposal](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/blob/62bf76a9/project-docs/proposals/project-view.md#health): Health before Actions, and a note and an action beside each count. A group's head says once when its counts are from, as each count's note said it (not checked yet, checking, checked at a time, out of date and why, or couldn't finish), and carries the action that brings them current while they aren't, so the four counts from Check Every Build show one note and one **Check Every Build**. Each count is one line, its label then its value, the label wrapping in its own column. Overdue reviews keep their day: when the check is current but from another day, that count alone is a dash, with its own note and the check. "To do" counts the current counts above zero; a group that isn't current isn't a thing to do, since its head says so, with its action. A dash is still named in words, by its group's note, never by color alone (decision 46). Actions stay open at first. _Why:_ a count's label, value, note, and action wrapped each at its own indent, so every count read differently; four counts from one check repeated one note and one button and counted as four things to do though one check brings all four current; and under Health, whose height changes as the counts do, the actions moved. _From:_ the maintainer, from a screenshot of 0.4.1's view, 21 October 2026, in [#777](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/777).
