section: The binary
---

**`ascribe outline` and its MCP tool, `ascribe refs`, and the editor bring a memo file to what it's about, and the commands' JSON gains keys without moving a `schema_version`.** `ascribe outline` lists the memos about a page under it, and those about a section or a block in it under its heading, in path order, each with its first line, and marks one outdated or personal; its JSON has `memos` on the page and on each heading, each entry `file`, `state` (`placed` or `outdated`), `quote`, `first_line`, and `personal`. A detached memo isn't listed there. `ascribe_outline` returns the same. `ascribe refs` lists a memo file as a place, with `use` `memo`, at its `about` line, and `personal`, a detached one under the target it names, as [decision #533](533-memo-files-in-spec.md) says. In VS Code, a CodeLens above a heading or a block counts its memos ("3 memos, 1 outdated") and opens them, and one at the top of the page counts the page's; **This page** has a Memos section, listing the memos about the heading at the cursor, or the page. **Ascribe: Add a memo** and **Ascribe: Add a personal memo** write a file about the section at the cursor, by its id, or about the page above its first heading with an id, quoting the selection or the first line of the block at the cursor, named from the quote's first words or the heading's id, never over a file that's there, and open it at its text. In a memo file, `about` completes the project's targets and links to what it names. The actions' labels are sentences, as the editor's checklist asks, not the phase file's "Add Memo". _Why:_ a reason is kept so that whoever is about to change a block meets it there; listing memos where pages and targets are already listed, and adding keys rather than changing them, brings memos to every reader of those commands without breaking one. _From:_ the maintainer, on the memos plan ([README](../memos/README.md), decisions 17 and 25, the latter from [phase 1](../memos/phase-1-decisions.md), and [phase 6](../memos/phase-6-editor.md)), 14 and 15 October 2026, as [#557](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/pull/557) and [#561](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/pull/561) shipped them on 17 October 2026; recorded when the plan closed, with [#536](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/536).
