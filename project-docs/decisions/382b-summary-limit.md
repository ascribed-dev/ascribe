**An entry's bold sentence is at most 100 characters, as it reads.** `changes.test.ts` holds new entries in `changes/` to it, counting the text without its Markdown; the entries already in `CHANGELOG.md` aren't rewritten. _Why:_ the changelog page shows each entry closed as its bold sentence, and 100 characters is about two lines at the page's measure; a third of the sentences ran past that, so a closed entry wasn't scannable. _From:_ issue #382, 12 October 2026.
