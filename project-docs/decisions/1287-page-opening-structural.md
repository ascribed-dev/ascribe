---
section: The language
---

**`page-opening-missing` is structural, and on for every project: it reports a page whose first block after its title isn't a paragraph written in the page, such as an `@include`, a heading, a list, a table, or code, and never judges the paragraph's words.** A level-1 heading that repeats the title, a memo, an HTML comment, and a directive bound to the block below it, such as `@summary` or `@available`, are passed over; fragments aren't checked, and `[checks]` sets its level or turns it off. Openings that are only a pointer, such as "Part of …", are left to a project's prose rules, as this repository's Vale rules hold them. So that a page started from Ascribe's templates opens with a paragraph, the conversion of The Good Docs Project's templates takes off the heading their how-to, tutorial, and reference templates open with: `## Overview`, which the title already does the work of, is removed, and `## {Reference description}`, a placeholder, becomes a paragraph of its words (the rule `opening` in `scripts/templates/rules.toml`). _Why:_ telling a sentence from a fragment with no main verb needs parsing English, and a check every project's pages get, in any language, can't guess at it without being noisy; and a check on by default can't flag the pages Ascribe's own templates start. _From:_ Kyle, [#1287](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/1287), 3 and 4 November 2026.
