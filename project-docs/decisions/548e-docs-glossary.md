section: The docs and the site
---

**Ascribe's docs define each of their terms once, on their glossary page, and a how-to in the content model's folder teaches a writer to set one up.** Where the page is, and its place in the sidebar, are [decision #545a](545a-glossary-pages.md)'s. `docs/content/glossary.md` has a section for each term the docs use: a sentence or two that defines it, then a link on to the page that documents it. Their terms that are ordinary words too, build, phrase, and variant, are `marked`, and the pages link the occurrences that mean them to their headings there. **Set up a glossary**, `docs/content/content-model/glossary.md`, type `how-to`, sits beside the glossary's reference, as [decision #452](452-docs-by-feature.md) puts a feature's pages together; it links each reference rather than repeating it, takes the docs' own glossary as its example, and is linked from `@term` in the directives reference, `[glossary]` in the content model reference, the editor's page, and the docs' home page. The Ascribe skill's guidance for writing pages says the same to agents: a term is defined once, on a glossary page, and the first sentence of its definition is what a hover shows. _Why:_ a reader who follows a term should land on a definition, not partway down a reference page, and the docs show a writer the glossary they teach. _From:_ the maintainer, on the glossary pages plan ([README](../glossary-pages/README.md) decisions 14 and 20, and [phase 5](../glossary-pages/phase-5-guide.md)'s design), 14 to 16 October 2026, built in [#593](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/pull/593) and [#601](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/pull/601); recorded when the plan closed, with [#548](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/548).
