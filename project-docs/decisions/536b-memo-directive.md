---
section: The language
---

**`@memo` is a built-in directive (SPEC §4.10), a sibling of `@intended` and not an extension of it: a line form whose text is a line primary, and a raw container form whose lines nothing parses.** It takes no attributes, and its line form's text is required. What a memo is about follows `@available`'s rule: at the top of a section, the section; anywhere else, the block below it, touching it. Its container binds as its line form does, measured from its `@end`, so the block it's about starts on the line after `@end` (SPEC §3.8, "A container that binds"); `@memo` is the one built-in whose container binds. Memos stack with each other and with following-block directives, in either order, so `@available` above a memo binds the block below the memo. A container memo is raw (SPEC §3.5): the fork's scanner keeps the lines between `@memo:` and a line that's only `@end` at the opener's indent as text, as a fenced code block's are, with the prefixes of the container it's in, so nothing inside is a block, a directive, an inline, or a heading, and a fence inside that doesn't close changes nothing around it; `@memo` is the one built-in with a raw container, and `crates/comrak-ascribe/FORK.md` lists the change. A line memo with no text is `directive-primary`, an attribute is `attribute-unknown-key`, and a memo with nothing after it, outside the top of a section, is `binding-no-block`: the errors any directive gets. An empty container is advice, [decision #551](551-empty-memo-advice.md). `ascribe fmt` treats a memo as it treats `@intended`: the directive touches its block, a closed container follows the blank-line rule, and the text, a container's lines included, is left as written. _Why:_ a reason is about one place in a page, and `@available`'s rule already says where that is; a raw container makes "nothing reads a memo" true by construction, where parsed contents would need every reader of the tree to remember to skip them. _From:_ the maintainer, on the memos plan ([README](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/blob/395fe80d/project-docs/memos/README.md), decisions 3, 4, 8, 21, and 23's last sentence, the last two from [phase 1](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/blob/395fe80d/project-docs/memos/phase-1-decisions.md)), 14 and 15 October 2026, as [#549](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/pull/549) shipped them on 16 October 2026; recorded when the plan closed, with [#536](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/536).
