---
section: The language
---

**A glossary is a page: a term is a heading marked `@term` on a page `[glossary] pages` lists, and `[glossary.terms]` is gone.** `@term` is a built-in line directive at the top of a section, with no primary and the attributes `aliases` (a string, `|` between forms), `match` (`first`, `every`, or `marked`), and `case-sensitive`; it's SPEC §4.11, after `@memo`, so no section is renumbered. A term's text is its heading's, its id the heading's source id, and its definition the paragraph that opens its section, as plain text, resolved for each build. `[glossary]` takes `match`, `case-sensitive`, and `pages`; `terms` is `model-unknown-key`, whose message names `pages` and `@term`. A path in `pages` that isn't there, or is a fragment, is `model-glossary-page` (ASC238), and two terms or aliases with the same text are `glossary-term-duplicate` (ASC239), at the second. `model-glossary-duplicate-term` (ASC098), `model-glossary-link` (ASC099), and `glossary-link-target-missing` (ASC203) are deleted, as decision #457a says, leaving gaps. A link an author writes to a term's heading is a use of the term: it carries `data-ascribe-term` and the definition as its title, `glossary-term-unused` counts it, and `ascribe refs term:` lists it, so a `marked` term with no such link is reported. `ascribe model` lists a term as `id`, `term`, `aliases`, `match`, `case_sensitive`, `page`, and `line`, at `schema_version` 3, and the JSON output's glossary uses gain `definition`. Ascribe's docs keep their glossary at `docs/content/glossary.md`, in the sidebar's Start section. It replaces [decision #468](468-glossary-link-heading.md). _Why:_ a definition written in `ascribe.toml` and again on the page a term links to drifted, couldn't use a phrase or `@available`, and filled `ascribe.toml` with prose; on a page it's written once, where the rest of the docs are. _From:_ the [glossary pages plan](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/blob/395fe80d/project-docs/glossary-pages/README.md), decisions 1 to 21, and its [phase 2](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/blob/395fe80d/project-docs/glossary-pages/phase-2-thread.md), on [#545](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/545), 17 October 2026.
