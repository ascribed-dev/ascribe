---
section: The language
---

**HTML comments are kept as they are, and one in a page or a fragment is advice, `html-comment` (ASC223), whose fix makes it a memo.** The outputs carry a comment as before. The check is a review check, configurable, reported at each comment, as a block or inside a paragraph, and not in a code block or a memo; a comment written for another tool is acknowledged with `@intended {check=html-comment}` above its block. Vale's own comments, those whose text starts with the word `vale`, are passed to Vale and never reported. **Rewrite as a memo** is the editor's fix for a comment that's a block of its own, and is unsafe, so `ascribe check --fix` never applies it: one line becomes a line memo and more a container, about the block below it, or at the top of the section when the comment is the section's last block; a comment at the end of a list item, or before a page's first heading, gets no fix. Comments are rewritten one at a time; nothing converts a project's in bulk. The docs' generated fragments say what generates them, and how to rewrite them, in a `@memo` touching their first block, or at the top of the first section of a fragment that opens with a heading, not in an HTML comment. _Why:_ a team writes HTML comments taking them for private, and they're in every published page's source and the JSON output, and a reworded one is a change to `ascribe diff`; leaving them in the outputs changes nothing for a project that means them, and the advice says where a private note belongs. _From:_ the maintainer, on the memos plan ([README](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/blob/395fe80d/project-docs/memos/README.md), decisions 9 and 23, the latter from [phase 1](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/blob/395fe80d/project-docs/memos/phase-1-decisions.md)), 14 and 15 October 2026, as [#552](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/pull/552) shipped them on 16 October 2026; recorded when the plan closed, with [#536](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/536).
