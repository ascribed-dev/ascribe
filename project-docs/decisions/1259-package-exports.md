section: The npm packages
---

**What `@ascribed/astro` and `@ascribed/elements` export is their API, and what isn't meant to be leaves the exports or moves under `internal/`.** `pageVersions`, `lineVersions`, and `versionNotice` stay in `@ascribed/astro/content` and are documented beside `sidebar()` and `neighbors()`: they're what a layout with its own version switcher or banner needs, and the package's `Versions.astro` and `VersionBanner.astro` are built on them. `@ascribed/astro/permalinks` and `@ascribed/astro/redirects` become `@ascribed/astro/internal/permalinks` and `@ascribed/astro/internal/redirects`, from `packages/astro/src/internal/`: they're exported only because the package's own route components (`Permalink.astro`, `MissingPermalink.astro`, `Gone.astro`) ship as source and import them by the package's name, and the Astro reference says once that `internal/` isn't part of the API. `@ascribed/elements` stops exporting `AscribeTabs`, `AscribeTab`, and `AscribeGroup`; it registers them itself, and `choose` stays. _Why:_ a published package's API is a contract, and these exports had no page: each is either documented or taken out of the API, so nothing is a contract by accident. The elements' contract is their markup and behavior ([CONTRACT.md](../../packages/elements/CONTRACT.md)), not their classes, which nothing imports; and nobody imports the permalinks' or redirects' modules but the package itself, so there are no users to move. _From:_ Kyle, in [#1259](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/1259), 3 November 2026, from the docs review of that day.
