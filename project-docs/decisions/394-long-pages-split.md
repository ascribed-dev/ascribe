section: The docs and the site
---

**A docs page over the delivery spec's page size is split into pages in a folder of its name, and a contract split that way keeps its wording.** `docs/content/reference/cli.md` keeps what every command shares and lists the pages in `docs/content/reference/cli/`, each holding whole sections of the old page; the same goes for the diagnostics reference, the `ascribe.toml` reference and its contract, the JSON report contract, and the editor guide. A split contract's sections, their numbers, and their headings' ids are unchanged, and it says no more than before except one line on each new page saying which contract it's part of, and a list of its pages on the first; that isn't a change to the contract. Every link into a page that was split leads to the page its heading is on now, and the permalinks follow with `ascribe permalinks sync`. Generated sections change at their generator: the diagnostics reference's source-file entries are two fragments, split at `FIRST_ON_PAGES` in `tests/conformance/tests/docs.rs`, and its headings start at level 2. The sidebar lists a page in a folder of a listed page's name under it. _Why:_ an agent's fetch cuts a page off at around 100,000 characters, and `page-size` reported six pages over the spec's 50,000; it can't be acknowledged, and raising the docs' limit would hide what it's for. _From:_ issue #394, split out of #366 on the maintainer's call in the review of #388, 12 October 2026.
