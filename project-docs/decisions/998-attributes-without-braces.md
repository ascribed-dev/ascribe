---
section: The language
---

**Attributes written without their braces (`@note type=warning: text`) are one error, `directive-extra-text`, whose message says the attributes need braces and whose fix adds them.** The message and the brace fix apply when the extra text starts with `key=value` pairs whose keys are all attributes of the directive; the fix is safe when a `:` and a primary follow the pairs, and unsafe otherwise. Other extra text keeps its message and its unsafe "Remove" fix. A line with extra text has no binding problem reported (`binding-heading` and its kin) and no required attribute reported missing. One case is recovered further: when the extra text is pairs whose keys are all the directive's attributes, followed by a trailing colon, and the directive has a container form that isn't raw, the line opens that container, so its `@end` matches; the line is still an error, and the pairs aren't read as its attributes. SPEC §3.1 says so, and its trailing-colon rule names the exception. _Why:_ leaving out the braces is the most natural mistake in the language, and the errors that followed from it sent the writer the wrong way, while the only fix offered deleted the note's text. _From:_ Kyle, [#998](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/998), 26 October 2026.
