section: The language
---

**A folder's name as a label has its `-` and `_` as spaces, everywhere.** A section with no `label` in `ascribe.nav` and no page of its own is named by its folder's last segment with `-` and `_` as spaces and its first letter capitalized, so `getting-started/` is "Getting started" in `nav.json`, `ascribe model`, a sidebar, an archive, and `llms.txt` alike. One function, `ascribe_resolve::navigation::folder_label`, makes the name, and `llms.txt` calls it. Before, `llms.txt` turned `-` and `_` into spaces and the rest kept them, so a reader saw two names for one folder. The outputs that change are each such folder's label in `nav.json`; in the comparison, `examples/templates: ascribe build/site/json/_ascribe/nav.json`, `examples/templates: ascribe build/site/site/_ascribe/nav.json`, and `examples/templates: ascribe build --emit site --anchors/site/site/_ascribe/nav.json`, each named with `--accept` in the pull request. _Why:_ "Getting started" is the name a reader expects, and it's what `llms.txt` already showed. _From:_ the maintainer, on [#585](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/585), found in review of [#584](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/pull/584), 17 October 2026.
