section: The binary
---

**The editor's Pages view is the project's navigation, and putting pages in order there, or moving and renaming them anywhere, edits `ascribe.nav` as typing would.** Pages shows **Start**, then each section in order by its label, its pages by theirs, then the fragments by folder. Dragging a section among its siblings, or a page within its section, writes `ascribe.nav`: the file opens with the change unsaved and its comments kept, **Undo** takes it back, the file too when the drag made it, and the edit is refused unless the navigation it leaves has the order asked for. Dropping a page on another section is a file move, asked first, since a section is a folder and the page's address changes. A page left out of a section that lists `pages` is marked **not placed**, with **Place Here**; **Rename Label…** sets a section's `label` in `ascribe.nav`, or a page's `nav-label` in its frontmatter. `ascribe rename`, and renaming or moving a file or folder in the editor, update `ascribe.nav` with the links: a page's entry in its section's `pages`, taken out when it leaves the folder, and a folder's `folder`; a section written inline is left as it is. The language server reads an open `ascribe.nav` as it's typed, reports its problems there with their fixes, and completes its keys, folders, and pages, and the extension gives `ascribe.nav`, `ascribe.permalinks`, and `ascribe.lock` VS Code's `toml` language id, colored only where a TOML extension is installed. _Why:_ the order is easiest to change where it's seen, and a move or a rename that left the file naming an old path would hand the writer an error they didn't make. _From:_ the maintainer, on the navigation plan ([README](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/blob/395fe80d/project-docs/navigation/README.md), decisions 1 and 15), 12 October 2026; built in [#577](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/pull/577), [#583](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/pull/583), and [#592](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/pull/592), 17 October 2026; recorded when the plan closed, with [#541](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/541).
