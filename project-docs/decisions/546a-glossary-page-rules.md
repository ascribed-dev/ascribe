---
section: The language
---

**A glossary page's mistakes are reported where they're written, a term follows its section in each build, and an unused term is acknowledged at its heading.** A term whose section doesn't open with a paragraph is `glossary-definition-missing` (ASC240), an error at its heading; the opening paragraph is found after the heading's directives and `@available`, `@intended`, and `@memo`, may come from a fragment, and, where the section opens with `@variant` groups, is each arm's own. A definition over `DESCRIPTION_LIMIT`, 300 characters, as a build derives it, is `glossary-definition-long` (ASC241), advice at the heading, page level, so it names the builds where it's over. `@term` on a heading no glossary page shows is `term-off-glossary-page` (ASC242), an error at the directive; on a page its fix adds the page to `[glossary] pages` in `ascribe.toml`, keeping the file as written, and is unsafe, so `ascribe check --fix` never applies it. A build that removes a term's section, or only its paragraph, doesn't link the term. `@intended` binds the heading at the top of a section, as `@available` does, and there covers the problems reported at the heading, and, as before, those in the block directly under the line when no blank line parts them, so a section's first block keeps its place to be acknowledged; `glossary-term-unused` is acknowledged that way, its place `block`, and `term` leaves `[[intended]]`, where it's `model-unknown-key` with a message that says where to write it. _Why:_ a term's mistakes are made on its page, so that's where they're seen and fixed, and a term that a build can't show shouldn't be linked by it. _From:_ the [glossary pages plan](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/blob/395fe80d/project-docs/glossary-pages/README.md), decisions 6, 8, 9, 15, 17, and 19, and its [phase 3](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/blob/395fe80d/project-docs/glossary-pages/phase-3-rules.md), on [#546](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/546), 17 October 2026.
