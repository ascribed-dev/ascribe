---
section: The language
---

**`ascribe check` reports a memo file's problems on the memo file, at its `about` or `quote` line, with four diagnostics, and reads the team's memos only.** `memo-invalid` (ASC225), an error whose next step is `write`, is a memo file that can't be read, has no frontmatter or frontmatter that isn't valid YAML, has no `about`, has an `about` that isn't written as a target, has a key besides `about` and `quote`, or has a `quote` with a target that has no text, an entry of `ascribe.toml`; with the variant `content-root`, it's `memos/` inside the content root. `memo-detached` (ASC226), a warning, `choose`, is an `about` that names nothing, and names the page's ids when a heading is what's missing. `memo-outdated` (ASC227), advice, `write`, is a quote no block holds. `memo-long` (ASC228), advice, `write`, is a body over `[checks.memo-long] limit` characters, 2,000 by default. The two pieces of advice are configurable in `[checks]`; the error and the warning are rows of SPEC §8.2, as [decision #533](533-memo-files-in-spec.md) says. `ascribe check` reads `memos/` only, so what it reports is the same on every machine, and the editor shows a memo file's problems as you type, a personal memo's included. An empty container memo in a page is [decision #551](551-empty-memo-advice.md)'s. _Why:_ a memo's mistakes are made in the memo file, so that's where they're reported and fixed; a detached memo is about nothing and is worth a warning, while words that changed under a quote, or a long memo, are for a writer to judge. _From:_ the maintainer, on the memos plan ([README](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/blob/395fe80d/project-docs/memos/README.md), decisions 16 and 23, the latter from [phase 1](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/blob/395fe80d/project-docs/memos/phase-1-decisions.md)), 14 and 15 October 2026, as [#557](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/pull/557) and [#561](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/pull/561) shipped them on 17 October 2026; recorded when the plan closed, with [#536](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/536).
