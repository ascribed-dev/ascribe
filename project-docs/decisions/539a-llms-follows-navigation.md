section: The binary
---

**`llms.txt` and the agents' compact index follow the project's navigation, in every project, the default order included.** Their first section is the content root's own page and pages, headed by the root's label, **Start** unless `ascribe.nav` gives it another, in place of "Pages"; then one section for each top section of the navigation, in its order, headed by its label, with its nested sections' pages folded in, in reading order. A page there keeps its title, not its `nav-label`. The split by folder keeps its folders. Every project with a plain output changes its outputs once, each difference accepted file by file, and `ascribe agents sync` rewrites the index in a project's `AGENTS.md` in the new order the next time it runs. _Why:_ an agent and a reader find the pages in one order, and the order a project already has is the one to keep. _From:_ the maintainer, on the navigation plan ([phase 1](../navigation/phase-1-decisions.md), decisions 3 and 7; [README](../navigation/README.md) decisions 5 and 20), 16 October 2026; recorded with [#539](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/539).
