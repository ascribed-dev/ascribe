---
section: The language
---

**A page template's instructions to its writer are memos, named by their text in the conversion's rules, and its placeholders for the page's own text stay placeholders.** Each template's table in `scripts/templates/rules.toml` has a `memos` list of the paragraphs said to the writer, each named by its text, and `scripts/templates/convert.ts` writes each as `@memo:` with its words and without the braces, or as a container of its lines when a paragraph of more than one line runs over 160 characters. A memo takes its paragraph's place, touching the block after it; one that would be the last block of its section goes to the top of the section. A rule that doesn't find its text in exactly one paragraph, or a memo that would be the last block of a list item or of a page before its first heading, stops the script. A paragraph that says what to do, or whether a part is needed, is an instruction; one that names what goes in its place stays a placeholder, and `template-placeholder-left` reports it as before. The rule that a quoted paragraph is an instruction is gone, and a placeholder in a block quote is a placeholder like any other. The `new-page` prompt tells an agent that the `@memo` lines are the template's instructions, to follow and then delete or keep. A project's copies of the templates are its own, to change or not. A section a page leaves empty where its template gives only memos is [decision #558](558-template-section-empty.md)'s advice. _Why:_ an instruction written as a placeholder is published as written if it's left in a page, and a memo never is; naming each by its text keeps which paragraphs are instructions a list the maintainer reads, which the script holds to the templates. _From:_ the maintainer, on the memos plan ([README](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/blob/395fe80d/project-docs/memos/README.md), decisions 10 and 22, the latter from [phase 1](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/blob/395fe80d/project-docs/memos/phase-1-decisions.md)), taking up what the page templates plan's decisions 18 and 22 left to it, 14 and 15 October 2026, as [#556](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/pull/556) shipped them on 17 October 2026; recorded when the plan closed, with [#536](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/536).
