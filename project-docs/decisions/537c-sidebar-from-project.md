section: The npm packages
---

**`sidebar(entries, options)` draws the project's navigation, and a site's own list is the option `nav`, which overrides it.** Without `nav`, `@ascribed/astro` reads the build's `_ascribe/nav.json` and joins each page to its collection entry by source path, throwing on a page the collection has no entry for; the root's pages are the first group, and each top section a group of its own, with `sections` for the nested ones, and each page has its `label` and `formatted` label. A site that called `sidebar(nav, entries, options)` calls `sidebar(entries, { ...options, nav })`, checked as before. `examples/astro-site` has no list; the starter keeps its list, `packages/astro/starter/src/nav.ts`, passed as `nav`, until it stops writing one. _Why:_ one signature, with no test of an argument's shape, that reads as what it is: the site's list overriding the project's. _From:_ the maintainer, on the navigation plan ([phase 1](../navigation/phase-1-decisions.md), decision 2), 16 October 2026; recorded with [#537](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/537).
