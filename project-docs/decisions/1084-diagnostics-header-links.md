section: The docs and the site
---

**Each diagnostic entry's header line keeps its compact form, and each word in it links to its definition: the severity to `#severity`, the level to `#file-level-and-page-level`, the next step to `#what-to-do-next`, "configurable" to `#setting-a-checks-level`, and "acknowledged" to the glossary's `acknowledgement`.** The links made three pages of the diagnostics reference too long, so they're split first, each part whole and in its list's order, as [decision #876](876-content-checks-split.md) and [decision #896](896-content-model-split.md) split the others: the source-file diagnostics at `FIRST_ON_SOURCES`, onto `docs/content/checks/diagnostics/sources.md`; the content checks at `FIRST_ON_PROJECT_CHECKS`, onto `docs/content/checks/diagnostics/project-checks.md`; and the content model's `FIRST_ON_CONTENT_MODEL_BUILDS` moved from the consumer to `[releases]`. The moved entries keep their permalinks, and `ascribe.addresses` forwards their old addresses. _Why:_ the words of the header line are defined on the reference's own page, and a reader on an entry's page shouldn't have to know where; each link costs about 55 characters in the agent Markdown, which put the pages over `page-size`'s 50,000, and raising the limit would hide what it's for. _From:_ Kyle, 26 October 2026, in the #953 project thread, recorded in [issue #1084](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/1084).
