section: The language
---

**A section its own page places is no `nav-section-unplaced`, and the default order sorts titles as a reader sees them.** A nested section whose own page is beside its folder sits where that page sits among its parent's pages (decision 24 of the [navigation plan](../navigation/README.md)), whatever its `[[sections]]` table says, so `ascribe.nav` leaving it out isn't advice: the table the fix would append wouldn't move it. SPEC §7.8, the navigation reference, and `docs/content/navigation/order.md` say that the page places it and its table doesn't. And pages the default order sorts by title, and those a section's `pages` leaves out, are sorted by the title's plain text, with the phrases its type's `title` takes as the content model declares them (whatever line a build is for), without case, then as plain text, then as written: ``"`zeta.yaml` options"`` sorts under z and `{product} overview` under the product's name, where they sorted by the backtick and the brace. `Section::places` says which sections are placed, for the navigation and the check alike. No build output changes in the comparison: no example's titles sort differently. _Why:_ labels have been plain text since decision [#594](594-plain-text-labels.md), so an order by the markup put pages out of the order their labels read in; and advice whose fix changes nothing tells a writer to do something that does nothing. #610 decides the top level. _From:_ the maintainer, on [#613](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/613) and [#610](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/610), 18 October 2026.
