section: The language
---

**A top section is ordered among the top sections by `[[sections]]`, whether or not its own page sits beside its folder, and dragging it in Pages reorders it there.** At the content root, no section is placed by its page beside its folder: `guides/` with `guides.md` comes after Start's pages with the other top sections, in the file's order and then path order, where it used to take its place from where `guides.md` sorted among the root's pages, before every other top section, its `[[sections]]` table ignored. The page is still one of the root's `pages` in the navigation and `nav.json`, as the [navigation contract](../../docs/content/contracts/navigation.md) says, and naming it in the root's `pages` moves no section. Below the root, the [navigation plan](../navigation/README.md)'s decision 24 holds, now narrowed to it: `checks/diagnostics/` sits where its own page beside the folder sits among `checks/`'s pages, and dragging it in the Pages view moves that page in `checks/`'s `pages`. `Section::places` says which sections are placed, for the navigation, the editor's edits, and the Pages view's drags alike. SPEC §7.8, the navigation reference, [Put your pages in order](../../docs/content/navigation/order.md), and the editor's page say so. A project whose top section has its own page beside its folder may see its top sections in another order, in every sidebar, `nav.json`, `llms.txt`, and reading order. _Why:_ since #584 a top section's own page beside its folder is read with that section, in no list a writer sees among Start's, so nothing visible ordered the section, and the Pages view, which still drew it inside Start, disagreed with every sidebar. _From:_ the maintainer, on [#610](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/610), 18 October 2026.
