section: The look
---

**The mark is a heavy single-storey pixel a with a gutter's change marks beside it, in Bryo Studio's Deep River colors, drawn with the wordmark from the pixel grids in `design/mark.toml`.** The change marks split around the a's middle row, so they read as two changed lines and not as an l. The wordmark is the mark followed by "scribe" in the same letters, which exist for the wordmark only. The mark's colors are `color.brand` in `design/tokens.toml`: Deep River for dark and the mark's tiles, and Shallows, a light companion from the same hues, for a light page; their contrast is checked as the stylesheets' pairings are (decision 46). `scripts/design/assets.ts` draws every image of the mark from the grids, `design/mark.svg` and `design/wordmark.svg` among them, so the images' sources in decision 49 are the grids and the brand colors, not hand-made SVGs. The social cards' text is outlined from Charter Bold (`design/fonts/Charter-Bold.ttf`, Bitstream's license), which replaces Inter Display as decision 48's one pinned font. The pages' own colors don't change here. _Why:_ the mark says what [the brand story](../brand/story.md) promises, docs as code with the tools docs need, and the pixel grid draws it exactly at every icon size and makes it one family with its maker. _From:_ the maintainer, choosing among the boards in `design/ascribe.af`, 21 October 2026, in [#801](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/801); [the identity](../brand/identity.md) has the rest.
