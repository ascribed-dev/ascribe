section: The language
---

**A page that's `listing: unlisted` is left out of the navigation, and so out of every sidebar drawn from it.** This amends the search and answers plan's [decision 15](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/blob/5b22286fd2f370e796219e68a7c9d7cdb2ba8acc/project-docs/search-and-answers/README.md#decisions), which made `unlisted` about crawlers and agents: it now also keeps a page out of what readers see in the sidebar, and there's no second setting for that. The page is still published at its address. _Why:_ a page not offered to crawlers and agents is one the project doesn't point readers to either, and one setting is one thing to learn. _From:_ the maintainer, on the navigation plan ([phase 1](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/blob/395fe80d/project-docs/navigation/phase-1-decisions.md), decision 10, and the README's decisions 11 and 27), 16 October 2026; recorded with [#537](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/537).
