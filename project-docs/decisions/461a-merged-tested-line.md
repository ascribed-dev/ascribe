section: The language
---

**A tested line made from several observations says each entry once, with its distinct values in order, and the operating systems after `on`, always listed.** Values are in version order when every one is a dotted number, comparing each number in turn, and in text order ignoring case otherwise. Past three, an entry is how many there are and the lowest and highest: `Node, 5 versions from 18.20.0 to 24.9.0`, or `values` when they aren't versions. Two entries, one of which lists values, are parted by a comma too: `Node 22.4.0 and 24.9.0, and TypeScript 5.9.2 on Linux`. An observation with one environment gives the line it gave before. _Why:_ [phase 4](../tested-examples/phase-4-status.md) sets the merge and the count past three, and leaves open what a value that isn't a version is called and how two lists in a row read; "5 versions" of words that aren't versions would mislead, and without the comma "Node 22.4.0 and 24.9.0 and TypeScript 5.9.2" reads as three things of one kind. _From:_ the implementation of [issue #461](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/461), for Kyle's review in its pull request, 13 October 2026.
