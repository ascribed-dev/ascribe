section: The binary
---

**This page's About lists a page's old addresses as Also answers at rows, from `about.alsoAnswersAt` in the server's `ascribe/page` answer, each an address with the `ascribe.addresses` entry it comes from, as `redirects.json` writes them.** One row each, after Markdown, with the entry in its tooltip; a fragment has none. The server works them out with each comparison, in every build, so an unsaved entry counts and the answer costs nothing more. _Why:_ a writer moving or deleting a page needs to see what still leads to it, and the build's own redirects are the truth of that (phase 4's design, "The views"). _From:_ [#908](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/908), [redirects, phase 4](../redirects/phase-4-editor.md), 25 October 2026.
