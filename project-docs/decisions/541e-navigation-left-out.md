section: The language
---

**A page is in its folder's section and no other, and the navigation is sections and pages in order, with their labels, and nothing more.** To put a page in another section, move its file. Left out, each until a case needs it and a decision of its own adds it: a page in two sections, a section for a content type, an entry that isn't a page (an external link, a heading with no folder, a divider), an order for each build, weights in frontmatter, routes, a table of contents inside a page, generated pages and their place in a section, a version switcher, and a command that writes `ascribe.nav` from a site's own list, which a site moves over by hand. _Why:_ a section is a feature's folder (decision [#452](452-docs-by-feature.md)), so where a page's file is says where it belongs, once; each of the rest is a shape that the file, every output that carries the navigation, and every reader of it would have to keep. _From:_ the maintainer, on the navigation plan ([README](../navigation/README.md), decisions 6 and 17, with its "Carved out" and "Later" lists), 12 October 2026; recorded when the plan closed, with [#541](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/541).
