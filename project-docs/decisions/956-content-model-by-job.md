section: The docs and the site
---

**The `ascribe.toml` reference and its contract are grouped by job, a page each, and their sections are numbered in that order; SPEC §7 isn't reordered or renumbered.** The pages are what pages are (the reference's own page and the contract's, §1–§5), what pages hold (`content.md`, §6–§10), how content varies (`variation.md`, §11–§14), where pages are published (`output.md`, §15–§19), what pages draw from (`sources.md`, §20–§21), and the checks and the editor (`checks.md`, §22–§24, with the reference's §25), and the contract's validation rules keep §25 and §26 in `validation.md`. The JSON report contract's pages are grouped by the same jobs. An explicit id that moves to another page is recorded in `docs/ascribe.addresses`; a numbered heading's generated id isn't an address (SPEC §7.10), so an old link to one lands at the top of its page. _Why:_ newer sections went wherever there was room, so `checks.md` held `[permalinks]`, `[crawlers]`, `[listing]`, and `[apis]`, and a reader couldn't find a section by what it's for; SPEC's section numbers are cited across the diagnostics, decisions, and docs, so renumbering them is left for when the spec is frozen at 1.0. _From:_ Kyle, [#956](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/956), 26 October 2026.
