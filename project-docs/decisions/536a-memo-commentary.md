---
section: The language
---

**Commentary on a docs set is of three kinds, kept apart: a memo in the page, a memo file, and discussion in review; a memo is a reason, written by someone and kept for as long as it holds.** A memo has no replies, no thread, and nothing to resolve, and nothing about it is live: a question or a suggestion goes in review, and when the discussion there settles why a page is the way it is, that's kept as a memo. A memo is authored, not derived from anything, and shares nothing with a cache: deleting one loses it. A memo can't say it must be gone before the page is finished: it has no attribute for that and no to-do marker. The word is "memo" for both kinds, `@memo` in a page and a memo file in `memos/`; "note" stays the callout (`@note`, `[notes.<type>]`, `note:<type>`), and "comment" stays a review thread. _Why:_ the reason a page is written the way it is gets lost in a wiki or an old pull request, and kept beside what it's about it's met by whoever edits next, person or agent; mixed with discussion or with a to-do list, every memo would become something to answer or clear, and one word for one thing at two lengths, used nowhere else in Ascribe, keeps it apart from the callout and from review. _From:_ the maintainer, on the memos plan ([README](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/blob/395fe80d/project-docs/memos/README.md), decisions 1, 2, 18, and 20, from the [proposal](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/blob/395fe80d/project-docs/proposals/memos.md)), the word on 11 October 2026 and the rest on 14 October 2026; recorded when the plan closed, with [#536](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/536).
