section: The npm packages
---

**`@ascribed/astro` serves every line of a versioned build from one site and one collection, with no change to a project's entry in `projects`: an older line's entries have the line in front of their id, `3.4/install`, and each entry's data carries `line` and `current`.** The integration reads each line's site output with a loader of its own, `glob` once per line, and builds the older lines in a second `ascribe build`, after the first has written `versions.json`; that run reports only what it built, its errors still failing the build, and `ascribe check` reports every line's warnings. `astro build` and `astro dev` build every line, and `astro dev` rebuilds them all on a change; its review compares every line, and a page shows its own line's changes. The check that Astro's `base` holds the project's base path allows for a line's number. Each line's outputs for agents, with their headers, are published at its base path, and a permalink forwards to the current line. `Versions.astro` lists a page's lines when there's more than one to list: each live line that has the page linked to it, the page's own marked `aria-current="page"`; a live line without it, unlinked; and each archived line with a `url`, linked to it; a page's lines include those of the pages `replaces` and `replaced-by` lead to, and its words are properties (`label`, `missing`, `archived`). `VersionBanner.astro` writes an older line's banner, "Documentation for Self-managed 3.4. Support ends on 31 January 2027. See this page for 3.5.", and nothing on the current line; its link is the page's route in the current line from `versions.json`, or the page that takes its place there, and the current line's home otherwise, and its words and the date's `locale` are properties. The sidebar shows the entry's own line, `sidebar(entries, { entry })` (the signature [decision #537c](537c-sidebar-from-project.md) gives it), and a site's own list, passed as `nav`, is checked against every line's pages: a page of any line must be listed, and a listed file must be a page of some line. `npx @ascribed/astro init` writes the three into the layout. This replaces the plan's decision 37, under which `Versions.astro` rendered nothing for a page only its own line has. _Why:_ Astro's own loader reads one folder and the lines are separate folders; the lines aren't known until the current line's build writes the file, and naming a line that has left the list would fail the run; a page only one line has still has the other lines to list, while a list of one is no way across, so a project with one live line renders as it did; a permalink is the address a product prints, and should land on the newest docs; and one list for every line keeps the sidebar the same across them. _From:_ the maintainer, on the [versions plan](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/blob/395fe80d/project-docs/versions/README.md), decisions 30, 36, 38, 55, 56, and 67, 15 and 16 October 2026 (30 on [#494](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/494#issuecomment-6195368949); 36 and 38 confirmed with [#506](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/pull/506), 55 and 56 with [#515](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/pull/515), and 67 with [#514](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/pull/514)); recorded when the plan closed, with [#502](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/502).
