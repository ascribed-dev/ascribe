**`ascribe versions retire` counts a change to the retired target's own part of another build's availability lines as expected, so it needs no `--force`.** A page of a build that isn't a line of the target whose only change is the target's entry in its `Available:` lines, its frontmatter's `available`, and its `<ascribe-availability-target>` elements (its state and versions, with its label kept) is listed under its own heading, and in the JSON's new `expected`, apart from `outputs`, which keeps meaning the changes that need `--force` and stays at `schema_version` 1. Any other change still needs `--force`: a page added or removed, content gained or lost, the target leaving a line, or another target's part of the same line. So `versions retire "self-managed 3.4"` on `examples/quill` runs without `--force`, though its cloud build's line goes from `self-managed (preview, 3.4+)` to `self-managed (preview)`. _Why:_ `--force` guards against a retirement changing something you didn't mean to change, and a version leaving the target's part of a line is the retirement doing its job. Leaving other targets' versions out of a filtered build's lines would change every filtered build and drop what SPEC §9.3 keeps on purpose, and changing quill's cloud build would only hide the problem in the example. _From:_ Kyle, [#1050](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/1050), 26 October 2026.
