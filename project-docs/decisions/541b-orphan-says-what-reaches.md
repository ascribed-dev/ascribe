section: The binary
---

**`page-orphan` reports a page no other page links to or includes, as it did, and says what does reach it: only the sidebar, or, for a page that's `unlisted`, nothing but its address.** Ascribe knows the navigation, so the check no longer hedges that a menu may reach the page: its message, the docs, and the tooltip in the editor's **Pages** view each say what's so. A page in the navigation that only the sidebar reaches is still reported, since no prose points to it, and no second check splits that case from the `unlisted` one. It stays advice, configurable, and acknowledged in a page's `intended`, and an `index.md` and a build's only page still aren't reported. _Why:_ a fact in place of a hedge, keeping what the check is for, a page prose never points to, with no new name to learn. _From:_ the maintainer, on the navigation plan ([README](../navigation/README.md), decisions 14 and 26, and [phase 1](../navigation/phase-1-decisions.md), decision 9), 12 and 16 October 2026; built in [#577](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/pull/577), 17 October 2026; recorded when the plan closed, with [#541](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/541).
