section: The language
---

**A section a page leaves empty, where its template gives only memos, is advice of its own, `template-section-empty`, and a section with no template behind it isn't looked at.** On a page of a type with a template, a section is reported at its heading when the template has a section with the same heading, at the same level and with the same text, that has nothing under its heading before the next one but memos and directives bound to its heading, and the page's section has nothing in its whole extent, its subsections included, but memos, directives bound to its heading such as `@id`, and the subsections' headings. A heading followed directly by a subsection with text in it is filled. It's configurable in `[checks]` apart from `template-placeholder-left`, and has no fix. _Why:_ since #556 a template's instructions are memos, so these sections have no placeholder for `template-placeholder-left` to find, and an unfilled one builds as a heading with nothing under it. Scoping it to the template's own memo-only sections targets that gap and adds no advice to pages without a template, where a rule for what counts as empty is easy to get wrong. It's a diagnostic of its own, not a variant, because a placeholder left and a section left empty are different findings with different fixes, and a project may want one without the other; the registry's variants are other messages for one finding, which can't be set apart in `[checks]`. _From:_ triage, acting for the maintainer, on [#558](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/558), 17 October 2026; the details of what counts as empty from its implementor, for the maintainer's review.
