---
section: The language
---

**Nothing reads a memo's contents, and resolution removes a memo where it removes `@intended`, so no output, renderer, or comparison sees one.** In a memo, a `{key}` isn't a phrase, a link isn't checked and isn't a use of its target, an `@include` isn't followed, a `#` line isn't a heading and gets no id, and Vale isn't given the text; the source index, the checks, `ascribe refs`, and the language server find nothing in it. The editor colors a memo's text as a comment, in either form, with nothing in it colored as a link, a phrase, or a heading, and folds a container memo to its first line. Build resolution removes memos, in either form, with `@intended` (`drop_unpublished` in `crates/ascribe-resolve/src/build/mod.rs`), so the site, plain, and JSON outputs, both renderers, the preview, the HTML report, and the source anchors are what they'd be without the memo, and a memo in a fragment is removed from every page that includes it. A change to a memo alone is no change to `ascribe diff` or to review: comparing two versions compares what they publish, and the page isn't listed. No build option publishes a memo. _Why:_ a memo is for the people and agents who maintain a page, never for its readers, and a memo that's checked, indexed, or counted as a change would cost its writer something every time it's written or edited. _From:_ the maintainer, on the memos plan ([README](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/blob/395fe80d/project-docs/memos/README.md), decisions 5, 6, and 7), 14 October 2026, as [#549](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/pull/549) and [#552](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/pull/552) shipped them on 16 October 2026; recorded when the plan closed, with [#536](https://github.com/ascribed-dev/ascribe/issues/536).
