# The Ascribe sidebar

- **Project**: the project's language server and binary, and how many problems every build has, with when that was checked. It says when a change has made the count out of date, and **Check Every Build** brings it up to date. Its Actions build the project and sync its agent files, in the terminal.
- **Review**, while review is on: the changed pages, their problems, and the comments you haven't submitted.
- **This page**: the page you're on, headed by its title, type, and path, then its addresses, whether it's published, listed, due for review, over size, or unfinished, its problems, and what links to it or to the heading at the cursor. Only About starts open; open another section and it stays open on every page. A row that can warn says its state first, and the view's badge counts Health's things to do, so you see them with Health closed.
- **Pages**: the project's pages in the navigation's sections, and its fragments. **Orphans Only**, in its title bar, lists only the pages nothing links to.
- **Content model**: the project's page types, phrases, features, glossary terms, and the rest, with how many places use each.

The views show the project of the file you're in. In a workspace with several projects, **Switch Project** in Pages' title bar lists them all, with whether each one's language server is running, and shows another.

Opening the sidebar starts no language server: a project's server starts when you open one of its pages, or choose it in Switch Project.
