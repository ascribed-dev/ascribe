# The Ascribe sidebar

- **Review**, while review is on: the changed pages, their problems, and the comments you haven't submitted.
- **Projects**: every project in the workspace, and whether its language server is running.
- **This page**: the page you're on, its title, type, and addresses, whether it's published, listed, due for review, over size, or unfinished, its problems, and what links to it or to the heading at the cursor.
- **Pages**: the project's pages by type, its fragments, and pages nothing links to.
- **Content model**: the project's phrases, features, glossary terms, and the rest, with how many places use each.

Opening the sidebar starts no language server: a project's server starts when you open one of its pages.
