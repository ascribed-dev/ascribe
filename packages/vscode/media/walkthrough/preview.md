# The preview

The preview shows the page with the elements the published site uses: notes, steps, tabs for content that varies, and availability badges.

- It follows the editor: the page you open, your edits as you type, and where you scroll.
- Double-click a block in the preview to put the cursor on it.
- Its **Build** picker shows the page as each of the project's builds publishes it.

The preview button in a page's title bar opens it too.
