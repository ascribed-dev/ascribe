# The build lens

A build can keep one variant of content that varies, such as one edition's, and leave out what isn't available where it publishes. With the lens on, the editor dims what the build leaves out, and the hover says why:

> **Left out of self-hosted.** Shows only edition=self-hosted.

The status bar names the project and the build you're looking at, such as **docs · self-hosted**. Click it to switch the build: the preview and the lens follow.
