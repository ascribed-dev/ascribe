**Rules.** Each path in `pages` MUST be a page that's there, with its name's case as it is on disk (`model-glossary-page`). No two terms or aliases may be the same text, compared ignoring case unless both terms are case-sensitive: the second is reported (`glossary-term-duplicate`), and isn't a term. Since terms are read from pages, that's checked with the project, not by the loader.
