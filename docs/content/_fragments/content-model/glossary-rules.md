@available: ascribe 0.4.0
**Rules.** Each path in `pages` MUST be a page that's there, with its name's case as it is on disk (`model-glossary-page`). No two terms or aliases may be the same text, compared ignoring case unless both terms are case-sensitive: the second is reported (`glossary-term-duplicate`), and isn't a term. No two terms may have the same id, which two pages' headings with the same text give them: the second is reported the same way. A term whose section doesn't open with a paragraph has no definition (`glossary-definition-missing`), and one whose definition is over 300 characters in a build, the limit a page's `description` has, is advice (`glossary-definition-long`). `@term` on a page `pages` doesn't list, or in a fragment no such page includes, isn't a term (`term-off-glossary-page`); its fix lists the page, and the editor applies it, never `ascribe check --fix`, since it links the page's terms on every page. Since terms are read from pages, all of these are checked with the project, not by the loader.
