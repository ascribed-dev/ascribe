## Feature flag
@id: feature-flag

A named switch in your code that turns a behavior on or off without a deploy. Each one has a key, a type, and a default value.

## Rollout
@id: rollout

A plan for turning a flag on for a growing share of users: by percentage, by segment, or on a schedule.

## Segment
@id: segment

A saved group of users, matched by the attributes your app sends with each check.
