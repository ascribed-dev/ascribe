Your first week is for getting set up and shipping something small. Nobody expects you to know the codebase yet.

## Day one
@id: day-one

@steps
1. Pick up your laptop from the front desk and sign in with the password in your welcome email.
2. Set up your accounts:

   @include: ../_fragments/accounts.md

3. Join {help} and introduce yourself.

## Meet your team
@id: meet-your-team

Your manager sets up a one-on-one on your first day. Your onboarding buddy, named in your welcome email, can answer anything your manager can't.

These teams are the ones new engineers ask most often:

## Ship something
@id: ship-something

By the end of the week, merge one change to production. Your buddy has a starter issue for you: usually a small bug or a docs fix. It's how you learn [our release process](../engineering/releases.md) end to end.
