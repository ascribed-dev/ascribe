Every quarter, each team lead reviews who has access to their team's systems, and removes access nobody needs.

@note {type=policy}
Access that isn't confirmed in a review is removed two weeks after the review closes.

## What's reviewed
@id: whats-reviewed

- Production databases and their backups.
- Cloud accounts and their admin roles.
- The deploy dashboard.

## How to review
@id: how-to-review

@steps
1. Open the review issue the {team} team files for your team.
2. For each person, confirm the access, reduce it, or remove it.
3. Close the issue. The {team} team applies the changes.
