- **A removed page opens in the preview as it was.** Choosing a page the branch removes, in the Review view or in Changed Pages, shows it in the preview as it was at the base, with no Now side, where before the Review view opened its source diff and Changed Pages said there was no page to open. The language server's `ascribe/preview` answers for such a page too: with `review: true`, its `review` has the page as it was and its title. See [The Review view](docs/content/editor/index.md).
