- **`ascribe diff` lists a page the lines share once, with the lines it changed in.** The text says "in every line" or names the lines, and the HTML report lists the page once with a **Line** switch. In JSON each line stays a build of its own, its routes under the line's folder, and the site preview's review marks the changes of the line a page is in. See [Diff](docs/content/review/diff.md).
