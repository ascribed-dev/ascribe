- **`ascribe diff` says a change once when it's the same in every build.** A page that changed the same way in every build that publishes it is listed first, `keys.md: 1 changed, in every build`, instead of under each build, and each build lists its other pages. Builds that differ are still listed apart, and `--format json` and `--format html` are unchanged ([ascribe diff](docs/content/review/diff.md#cli-diff)).
