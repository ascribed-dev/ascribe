- **Behavior change: a glossary is a page, and `[glossary.terms]` is gone.** List the glossary's pages in `[glossary] pages`, and mark each term's heading on them with `@term`; the paragraph under the heading is the term's definition, which a built page shows as the title of each link to the term. `@term` takes `aliases`, separated by `|`, and its own `match` and `case-sensitive`. A `[glossary.terms]` table is now an error that says how to move it. A link you write to a term's heading is a use of the term, with its marker and its definition, and that's how a `marked` term is linked: one that no link names is reported by `glossary-term-unused`. Two terms with the same text are `glossary-term-duplicate`, and a path in `pages` that isn't a page is `model-glossary-page`; `model-glossary-duplicate-term`, `model-glossary-link`, and `glossary-link-target-missing` are gone. `ascribe model` lists each term with its page and line, at `schema_version` 3, and the JSON output lists each use's definition. See `docs/content/content-model/content.md` and `docs/content/writing/directives.md`.
