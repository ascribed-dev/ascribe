- **`ascribe check` reports a permalink with no summary, and a summary too long to show.** `summary-missing` is advice at the `@id` line of a heading whose `@id` marks it a permalink and whose section has no `@summary`; a project that ships embedded help raises it to a warning in `[checks]`, so every permalink needs one. `summary-long` is advice at a `@summary` whose text is 300 characters or more, as plain text on one line; `[checks.summary-long] limit` sets another length. A permalink or a summary in a fragment is reported once, in the fragment. A page's own permalink has its description, which `page-description-missing` already reports. See [Write the summaries](docs/content/permalinks/in-your-product.md).
