- **`template-section-empty` reports a template's section a page leaves empty.** A section a template gives only memos, such as the concept's "Use cases" or the troubleshooting template's "Symptom 1", has no placeholder, so one a page doesn't fill in builds as a heading with nothing under it, and `template-placeholder-left` can't find it. On a page of a type with a template, `ascribe check` now reports it as advice at its heading when the page's section, its subsections included, has nothing but memos, directives such as `@id`, and empty subsections' headings. It's configurable in `[checks]`. See the [templates guide](docs/content/templates/index.md#whats-left-to-fill-in).
