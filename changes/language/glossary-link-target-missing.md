- **A glossary term's `link` to a heading its page doesn't have is an error.** The new diagnostic `glossary-link-target-missing` (ASC203) is reported at the `link` in `ascribe.toml` when its `#id` isn't a heading's id on the page, written in it or in a fragment it includes, as `permalink-retired-target-missing` is for a retired permalink's `to`. Before, such a term was never linked, and `ascribe check` said nothing. See `docs/content/content-model/content.md` and [decision #468](project-docs/decisions/468-glossary-link-heading.md).
