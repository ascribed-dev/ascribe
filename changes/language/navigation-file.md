- **`ascribe.nav` orders a project's sections and pages, and every site output gains `_ascribe/nav.json`.** A section is a folder of the content root, and its own page is its `index.md` or the page beside the folder with its name. With no file, a project has the default order: sections in path order, the root first, and in each its own page, then its pages by type in `ascribe.toml`'s order, then by title. `ascribe.nav` lists sections, each with `folder`, and optionally `pages` and `label`, and what it leaves out keeps the default. A page that's `listing: unlisted` is left out of the navigation, and so out of the sidebar. A file that can't be read is `nav-invalid`. Every project's site output changes: each build's has `_ascribe/nav.json`, its share of the navigation, `generated` in its manifest, whether or not the project has the file. For now a `folder` or a `pages` entry that names nothing is passed over, and nothing reports it. See [Navigation](docs/content/navigation/index.md) and [the navigation contract](docs/content/contracts/navigation.md).
