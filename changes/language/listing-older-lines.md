- **`[listing] older-lines` keeps every line but the current one out of an index.** `older-lines = "unlisted"` makes each page of an older line `unlisted`, so it's `noindex` and left out of the sitemap and `llms.txt`; a page there that sets a looser `listing` is `listing-loosened-in-line`. See [Keep the older lines out of an index](docs/content/versions/index.md#keep-the-older-lines-out-of-an-index).
