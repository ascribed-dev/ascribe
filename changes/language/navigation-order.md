- **The default order sorts pages by their titles as a reader sees them.** A title read with code spans sorts without its backticks, and one that takes phrases with their values, so "`zeta.yaml` options" comes after "Apply" and `{product} overview` sorts by the product's name; two titles with the same text sort as written. A section whose own page sits beside its folder, placed where that page is, is no longer `nav-section-unplaced` when `ascribe.nav` lists a sibling and not it: its table wouldn't move it. See [Navigation](docs/content/navigation/index.md#the-default-order).
