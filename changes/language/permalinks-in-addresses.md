- **Behavior change: retired permalinks and aliases are kept in `ascribe.addresses`.** Each is an entry under the key `permalink:` and its name: `[retired]` with its reason, or `[moved]` to the page it leads to, or, for an alias, to `permalink:` and the name it leads to. `ascribe.toml`'s `[permalinks.retired]` and `[permalinks.aliases]` still work, with a warning, `model-permalinks-moved`, until `ascribe addresses init` moves them, which takes the tables out of `ascribe.toml`; a retired permalink with a `to` becomes a move, and keeps no reason. `ascribe permalinks retire` and the editor's quick fix write `ascribe.addresses`, making it when there's none, and `retire` takes `--reason` or `--to`, not both. `permalink-removed` is now `permalink-lost`. See `docs/content/permalinks/index.md`.
