- **Every build writes each permalink's section to `_ascribe/help.json`, for a product to show.** The site output has an entry for each permalink and each alias of one: its title, its summary, from the section's `@summary`, its section as the plain output writes it, with images as their alt text, and its address. A product bundles the file from its own line's build and shows a section in a tooltip or beside an error, with "Read more" opening the permalink ([Show the docs in your product](docs/content/permalinks/in-your-product.md), output-layout contract §1.7).
