- **A page template's instructions to the writer are memos, never published.** In the six templates `ascribe templates eject` writes, a paragraph that tells the writer what to do, such as `{This section is optional}`, is now `@memo: This section is optional`, at the top of its section or above the block it's about, so one left in a page is never seen by a reader. A paragraph that marks where the page's own text goes stays a placeholder. `template-placeholder-left` doesn't report a memo the page keeps, and no longer treats a paragraph in one of the template's block quotes as an instruction: a placeholder in a block quote is reported as any other is. The `new-page` prompt tells an agent to follow the memos. A project's own copies are its own: `ascribe templates status` says what changed in the set.
