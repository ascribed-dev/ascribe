- **`ascribe templates eject` writes a page template and its guide into a project.** It writes The Good Docs Project's how-to template, converted into an Ascribe page, and its guide into `templates/`, or `--to`'s folder, adds `[types.how-to]` to the end of `ascribe.toml` with its `template` and `guide`, and records what it copied in `templates/templates.lock`. It checks everything first, and writes nothing when a type is declared already or a file is in the way. `ascribe templates list` shows the set. See `docs/content/guides/templates.md`.
