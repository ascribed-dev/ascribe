- **`ascribe search` narrows to paths, a type, case, or whole words, and summarizes by page.** Paths after the query keep it to the pages there, a fragment's line counted only for them; `--type` keeps to a content type; `--case` matches case as written; `--word` matches only where a word or a phrase isn't inside a longer one. `--summary` lists the pages and how many places each shows, most first. When nothing matches, the last line says whether the words are in no page, written where no build searched publishes them (at the first place, and how many more), or, with `--build`, published by builds not searched, which it names. See `docs/content/command/questions.md`.
