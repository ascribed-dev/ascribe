- **`ascribe init` sends you to a first page, and `ascribe check` says when there are none yet.** `ascribe init`'s next step is now writing `docs/index.md` and running `ascribe check`, with the Astro guide after it. In a project with no pages and nothing to report, `ascribe check` says `No pages in docs/ yet.` in place of its summary, and exits with 0. See [`ascribe init`](docs/content/install.md).
