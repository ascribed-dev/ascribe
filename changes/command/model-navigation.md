- **`ascribe model --format json` reports the navigation.** Without `--section`, its `navigation` is the project's sections and pages in order, each section with its label, its own page, its pages, its nested sections, and `listed`, whether `ascribe.nav` lists it, and each page with its `source`, `label`, and `type`; a page that's `unlisted` isn't in it. `schema_version` stays `1`. See [`ascribe model`](docs/content/command/questions.md).
