- **`ascribe model` shows release lines, and `ascribe render` puts an older line under its folder.** The model's `releases` section lists each line with its status and the build that is it, and a versioned build says its target and line. `ascribe render --build site@3.4` and `ascribe_render` give the line's route, such as `/3.4/install/`, with its links under the same folder. See [Versions](docs/content/versions/index.md#in-the-editor-in-review-and-in-the-tools).
