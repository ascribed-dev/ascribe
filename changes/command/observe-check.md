- **`ascribe observe --check` says whether an observation still matches, and runs nothing.** It exits 1 when the observation is stale against the folder, for a pre-commit hook, and starts no program. A run of `ascribe observe` says what changed since the observation it replaces, `ascribe report`'s inventory counts each source's examples as current, stale, and unobserved, a source in another repository's from its copies, and `ascribe model` names each source's observations and their environments.
