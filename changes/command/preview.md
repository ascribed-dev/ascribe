- **`ascribe preview` shows a build's pages as a site, with no Node, editor, or Astro.** It checks the build and prints the problems as `ascribe check` does, renders the pages with the archive's renderer into `.ascribe/preview/`, errors or not, serves them at `127.0.0.1`, and renders them again as the pages and `ascribe.toml` change. See [`ascribe preview`](docs/content/checks/check.md).
