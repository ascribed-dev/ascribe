- **`ascribe search --format json` writes the places as a document of its own.** Each place has its `file`, `line`, and `column`, what it is (`in`), the block's whole `text` and where the `match` is in it, in characters, what it reads `through` (a phrase, a link's borrowed title, a snippet's code), its `arm`, and the first three `pages` that show it, each with its title, heading, id, and builds. The document has the totals, `truncated` and `next_command` when the list is cut at 500, the pages with `--summary`, and, when nothing matched, `source_places` and `not_searched_builds`. Its schema is `schemas/search.schema.json`. See `docs/content/command/questions.md`.
