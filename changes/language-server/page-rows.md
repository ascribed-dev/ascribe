- **`ascribe/page` answers whether a page is published, listed, due, over size, or unfinished.** Its `about` adds the page's description, review date, availability, builds, listing, permalinks, and template, and the answer adds `health` (its size, as `page-size` measures it, and its tested examples) and `includes`; each page in `ascribe/inventory` adds the `builds` that publish it.
