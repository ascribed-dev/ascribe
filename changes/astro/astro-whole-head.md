- **`Head.astro` writes everything a page's head says about the page, from its record.** Besides the title, description, canonical link, and robots rule, it writes `og:title`, `og:description`, `og:url`, and `og:type` from the same record, a `rel="alternate" type="text/markdown"` link to the page's Markdown version when the build publishes one and the page isn't `unlisted`, and the page's breadcrumbs as `BreadcrumbList` structured data. Two new properties carry what the site knows: `name`, the site's name, for `og:site_name` and, on the page at the host's root, the site-name structured data; and `card`, the page's social card, for the `og:image` tags. Each page's record in `_ascribe/pages.json` has its `breadcrumbs`: the page at each folder of its route, by title, down to the page itself. `astro dev` warns once about a tag `Head.astro` writes that the layout writes too. See the [Astro guide](docs/content/guides/astro.md).
