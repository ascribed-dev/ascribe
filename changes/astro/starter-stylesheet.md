- **`init` writes a stylesheet for the starter's layout.** `npx @ascribed/astro init` also writes `src/styles/docs.css`, which the layout imports: a readable column, the sidebar beside the page or behind a **Menu** on a phone, the table of contents beside the page where there's room, both color schemes, and spaced previous and next links. Its colors and spaces are the element library's `--ascribe-*` properties, and deleting the file and its import leaves the layout unstyled, as before ([Set up a site](docs/content/astro/index.md#the-stylesheet)). An existing site keeps its own files; `init` never writes over one.
- **`MissingPermalink` no longer offers search.** Its default last paragraph offers the home page alone, since a site may have no search; a site with search passes its own paragraph as children ([the reference](docs/content/astro/reference.md)).
