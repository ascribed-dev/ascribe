- **`@ascribed/astro` writes a page's head, the sitemap, and `robots.txt` from the page records.** A layout includes `<Head entry={entry} />`, from `@ascribed/astro/Head.astro`, for the page's `<title>`, description, canonical link, and `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">` when the page is `listing: unlisted`; its `title` property is a function from the page's title and its record to the whole title. The site gets one `sitemap.xml`, at Astro's `base`, with every listed page of every project and every other page Astro built, less permalinks' forwarding pages, the not-found page, and the routes in the new `sitemap: { exclude }` option, and one `robots.txt`, at the host's root, with each project's `[crawlers] lines` and the sitemap's address, in `astro build` and `astro dev`. A site with its own `public/robots.txt` keeps it, a site whose `base` isn't `/` gets no `robots.txt` and the lines its host's needs in the build's output, and a site with no `site` gets no sitemap and a warning. The layout `npx @ascribed/astro init` writes includes `Head.astro`. See the [Astro guide](docs/content/guides/astro.md).
