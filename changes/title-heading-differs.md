- **`ascribe check` reports a page whose own top heading says something its title doesn't (`title-heading-differs`).** A page that writes its own level-1 heading, as a landing page does in its hero, is reported at its title when neither contains the other, ignoring case, since a search engine may show either as the page's name. Most pages write none, since the layout draws the title, and aren't looked at. It's advice that may be fine as it is: acknowledge it in the page's `intended` frontmatter. See [the diagnostics reference](docs/content/reference/diagnostics.md).
