- **Fixed:** a page `ascribe rename` moves takes its glossary links in `ascribe.toml` along. So do the retired permalinks whose `to` names it, and the editor's move does the same. Before, a glossary term's `link` kept the old path, so the model had an error and every later rename refused. Each keeps its `#id`, and `ascribe.toml` keeps its comments and order.
