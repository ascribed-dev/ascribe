- **Fixed:** a rename keeps every memo about what it renames attached. Renaming a glossary term's heading id rewrites the memos whose `about` is `term:<id>`, renaming a phrase rewrites those about `phrase:<key>`, and renaming a heading in a fragment rewrites those that name it through the page that includes it (`page.md#id`), as it does links. An `about` written as a block scalar or on the line under its key is rewritten too, where it was skipped; one written with an escape is written again on its key's line. `ascribe rename` and the editor's rename alike ([docs/content/writing/memo-files.md](docs/content/writing/memo-files.md)).
