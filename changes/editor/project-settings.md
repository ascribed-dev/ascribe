- **Project Settings shows and changes the settings in `ascribe.toml`.** **Ascribe: Open Project Settings** opens a tab of the project's `ascribe.toml` settings, each with its value, its default, its description, and the line that sets it: every simple setting, under its table, and every configurable check, with its level and, for `page-size`, `image-large`, and `memo-long`, its limit. A value is changed in place, unsaved, with the file's comments kept; one the file leaves out is added to its table; and **Reset to default** takes a setting's line out, with the comment that touches it. The tab follows the file as it's typed and undone. A search finds a setting by a word of its name, its key, or any sentence of its description, and **Set in ascribe.toml** keeps to what the file sets; content types, dimensions, releases, builds, and sources are cards that open the file at their table. The tab opens from `ascribe.toml`'s title bar too, on a project the extension found and not on a fixture's that `ascribe.projects.exclude` leaves out. See [Project Settings](docs/content/editor/settings.md#project-settings).
