- **Project Settings shows and changes the settings in `ascribe.toml`.** **Ascribe: Open Project Settings** opens a tab of the project's `ascribe.toml` settings, each with its value, its default, its description, and the line that sets it: every simple setting, under its table, and every configurable check, with its level and, for `page-size`, `image-large`, and `memo-long`, its limit. A value is changed in place, unsaved, with the file's comments kept; one the file leaves out is added to its table; and **Reset to default** takes a setting's line out, with the comment that touches it. The tab follows the file as it's typed and undone. See [Project Settings](docs/content/editor/settings.md#project-settings).
