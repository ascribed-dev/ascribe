- **New Page starts a page from its type's template, with the first placeholder selected.** **Ascribe: New Page**, in the Command Palette, the **Pages** view, and the Explorer's menu on a folder, asks for the type, each with what it's for, and the title, makes the page in the content root or in the folder, with `type:` in its frontmatter, and opens it with the first thing to fill in selected. **Ascribe: Go to Next Placeholder** steps through the rest.
