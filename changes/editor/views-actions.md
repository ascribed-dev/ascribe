- **Pages and Content model have menus and filters.** In **Pages**, a page with a problem is colored as in the Explorer, and one the status bar's build leaves out is dimmed. A page's menu opens its preview, copies its link, renames or moves it, finds its references, and reveals it in the Explorer; a section's starts a page in it, and **Orphans Only** lists what nothing links to or includes. **Content model** lists page types and each dimension's values. An entry's menu finds its references, renames a phrase or a value, and inserts a phrase or a widget at the cursor of the page you last had open; **Unused Only** lists what nothing uses. See [the editor](docs/content/editor/index.md#this-page-pages-and-content-model).
