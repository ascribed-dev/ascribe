- **The sidebar's This page view replaces Used by.** Its **About** section gives the active page's title, content type, address, and Markdown version's address, and its **Connections** section lists what links to and includes the page, or the heading at the cursor, with unsaved edits counted. It asks the language server once for each cursor move, review on or off, where Used by asked twice. See [This page, Pages, and Content model](docs/content/editor/index.md).
