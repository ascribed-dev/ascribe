- **The Pages view shows the navigation, and dragging in it orders your sections and pages.** **Pages** lists Start, then each section by its label with its pages in the order a sidebar shows them, then the fragments by folder, and selects the page you open. Drag a section or a page to put it before or after another, and the editor writes [`ascribe.nav`](docs/content/navigation/index.md), unsaved, keeping its comments; **Undo** takes it back. Drag a page onto another section and, once you say so, its file moves there with its links. A page the file leaves out is marked, with **Place Here** in its menu, and **Rename Label…** sets a section's label. The view no longer groups pages by type or lists orphans, which `ascribe check` reports. See [the editor](docs/content/editor/index.md#this-page-pages-and-content-model).
- **Moving a page or a folder keeps `ascribe.nav` right.** `ascribe rename` and a move in the editor rename a page's entry in its section's `pages`, or take it out when the page leaves its folder, and rename a moved folder's sections. Moving a folder in the editor also updates the links to the pages in it. See [`ascribe rename`](docs/content/command/questions.md#cli-rename).
