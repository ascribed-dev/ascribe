- **The editor shows a section's summary on its heading, and on its Permalink row.** The hover on a heading, or on its `@id` line, shows the section's `@summary` in the editor's build, as `_ascribe/help.json` writes it, and This page's Permalink row has it in its tooltip, or says the section has none. The language server's `ascribe/page` gives each permalink its `summary`. See [Writing in the editor](docs/content/editor/writing.md) and [the sidebar](docs/content/editor/sidebar.md).
