- **The editor says what a snippet's example was tested with.** A hover on a `@snippet`'s address shows its file, as a link that opens it, its region and lines, and, for a tested example, what it was tested with, "Changed after its last test run", or "Not tested", with the observations behind it and the folder to run `ascribe observe` in. `example-stale` and `example-unobserved` follow the source's observations and `ascribe.examples.toml` as they change on disk, without the page being edited, and offer **Copy the command**, which copies the line that runs `ascribe observe` there; the editor never runs it. A source's `ascribe.examples.toml` reports its problems as it's edited, and completes its keys. See `docs/content/editor/index.md`.
