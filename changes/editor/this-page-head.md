- **This page heads the page with its title, type, and path, and badges what needs doing.** The title draws its code spans as code where its type's `title` reads them. A row that can warn says its state first, such as an example's "changed since it was tested" before its address, with what it was tested with and the heading it's under after it. An open section's header stays at the top while you scroll its rows. The view's badge counts Health's things to do, so you see them with Health closed. When the page's language server failed to start, the view says so with **Show Output** and **Restart**, and in Restricted Mode it says Ascribe isn't checking the folder, with **Manage Workspace Trust**.
