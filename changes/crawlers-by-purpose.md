- **A project says what each kind of AI crawler may do, in `[crawlers]`: `training`, `search`, and `on-request`, each `"allow"` or `"disallow"`.** A disallowed purpose writes one group in `robots.txt` naming every crawler of that kind Ascribe knows, from their operators' own pages, with `Disallow: /`; `allow`, and leaving a purpose out, write nothing, so those crawlers stay under the lines for every crawler. The page records' `robots` gains `purposes` and `groups`, and `@ascribed/astro` writes the groups first; projects of one site that disagree on a purpose fail the build, naming both. A crawler named in two groups is an error (`model-crawlers-named-twice`). `listing-disallowed` now reads, for each search crawler, the one group that applies to it, so it reports a page that isn't listed when `search = "disallow"` or a group naming a search crawler keeps it from being fetched, and `crawlers-disallowed`, advice, says what disallowing `search` or `on-request` costs. The reference lists each crawler with its operator's statement, dated and linked ([the `ascribe.toml` reference](docs/content/reference/content-model.md), [the Astro guide](docs/content/guides/astro.md)).
