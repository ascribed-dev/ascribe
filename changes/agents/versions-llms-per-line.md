- **Each line of a versioned build has its own `llms.txt` and Markdown.** An older line's are at its base path, `/docs/3.4/llms.txt`, its index's heading names the line and points at the current line's, and `@ascribed/astro` publishes them. `ascribe agents index --build site@3.4` indexes a line, under a heading that names it; without `--build`, the current one.
