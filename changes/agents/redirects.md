- **Agents are told how to move pages without losing their addresses.** In a project with `ascribe.addresses`, `AGENTS.md` and the skill say to move a page with `ascribe rename`, to record a `git mv` with `ascribe addresses move --from-git`, and to forward or retire a page deleted on purpose. The MCP server's `ascribe_addresses_move` and `ascribe_addresses_retire` give the edit to apply. See [Agent instructions](docs/content/agents/instructions.md).
