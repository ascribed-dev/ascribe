- **`ascribe link` and the MCP tool `ascribe_link` answer a link to an OpenAPI operation with the operation.** Its `kind` is `operation`, and a new `operation` field has the API, the `operationId` or none, the method and path, the summary, the tag, whether it's deprecated, and its address in the reference; the text says the same, and the closest operations show their summaries. See [`ascribe link`](docs/content/command/questions.md).
