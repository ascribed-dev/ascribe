- **Agents are told to give a heading with a permalink a summary.** In a project whose headings declare permalinks, the rules `ascribe agents sync` writes say to give each section a `@summary`, the sentence a product shows with its help, and how to choose between `@summary` above a paragraph and `@summary: <text>`; the skill says so too. `ascribe check --format prompt` quotes the text of each section with no summary under its problem, so an agent can write the summary from the prompt.
